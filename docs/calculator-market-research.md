# Joff Tax calculator and tool market inventory

Snapshot: 7 October 2026. This is a live public-page inventory of [TaxTim's calculator hub](https://www.taxtim.com/za/calculators/), not a specification of every form field or proof that Joff Tax can file with SARS. TaxTim describes 29 general calculators, four specialist allowance calculators and three tools/trackers, **36 hub entries** in total. The hub says every calculator is unlimited and free without registration; calculations are estimates, distinct from a SARS assessment. Each linked row below is the actual destination reached from that hub. Inputs and outputs summarise public tool descriptions; a production formula and exact form schema must be checked against current SARS sources and the rendered interaction.

These are hub entries, not necessarily 36 independent calculation engines. Salary, bracket, bonus, net-to-gross and payroll share individual-rate logic; CGT/crypto share some gain and marginal-tax mechanics; section 11(e), 12C and 12E are distinct legal allowance regimes. The three final entries are guidance tools, not calculators. TaxTim also links a [vehicle logbook](https://www.taxtim.com/za/services/vehicle-logbook) and [expense tracker](https://www.taxtim.com/za/services/expense-tracker) outside this hub. Its separate [calculator mobile app](https://www.taxtim.com/za/services/taxtim-calculator-app/) advertises only salary, refund and retirement-lump-sum calculators, notifications and emailed results. Do not equate app coverage to hub coverage.

## Free versus paid boundary observed

[The hub](https://www.taxtim.com/za/calculators/) says every calculator is free without login or usage limit. [TaxTim's pricing page](https://www.taxtim.com/za/services/) says a free account also includes TimAI, document storage, deadline reminders, expense tracker, vehicle logbook, personalised tax report, bank statement importer and calculator app. Users can start and continue its return questionnaire without paying, but must pay before submission. Paid ITR12 tiers on that page are Lite **R215**, Smart **R565**, Ultra **R895** per return; individual IRP6 is **R600** per submission. Paid return features include plain-language guidance, human helpdesk, claimed 1,300+ checks, SARS data import, dashboard and direct SARS submission. The page says detailed human work beyond guidance can be separately priced. [Practitioner done-for-you filing](https://www.taxtim.com/za/services/let-our-human-experts-file-for-you) starts at **R1,299** and is a separate service. TaxTim's homepage displays a conflicting “ITR12 From R249” headline, so avoid an unqualified entry-price comparison.

Calculator parity therefore means a user can run the same **classes of estimates and planning tools**. It does not mean parity with TaxTim's actual return generation, SARS integration, practitioner help, audit handling or regulatory obligations. Joff Tax must describe its capabilities truthfully; no direct filing, SARS profile access or automatic data import can be claimed without real integration and authorisation. A calculator result is not a guaranteed refund, deduction, tax position or SARS decision.

## Original experience and monetisation hypotheses

Build one clear hub with search, situation filters, annual year selector and usable mobile forms for all 36 entries. A single optional profile can pass the same year, age and income between related calculations, while each tool still works immediately without login. Show a live result, step-by-step arithmetic, assumptions, source/year stamp, “what would change this result,” and the next relevant tool. Make the calculation inspectable and exportable; group related scenarios into a connected journey (salary → bonus → retirement contribution → refund preparation, or rental → provisional status → IRP6). This is a proposed differentiation, not a claim that competitors lack every feature.

Free can include **all 33 numeric/diagnostic calculators plus the three trackers**, transparent breakdowns, a basic share/export, and a document checklist. Charging for calculation alone would be hard to justify against TaxTim's unlimited free hub. Test a paid **Joff Tax Filing Readiness Pack** that reconciles a user's saved scenarios into one tax-year summary, flags inconsistent assumptions or missing records, organises supporting evidence with an audit trail, and creates a plain-language, reviewable eFiling preparation packet. This is a product hypothesis rather than verified demand. The pack should state clearly whether the customer files in SARS themselves. A later practitioner review or assisted filing must be scoped, staffed and priced separately by appropriately registered people. Do not present generic AI chat, storage or reminders as a unique paid advantage because TaxTim already advertises them in its free account.

Validate willingness to pay with a clear free-to-paid boundary and a small paid pilot; measure scenario completion, return visits, packet purchase, successful self-reported handoff and support load. Revenue, CAC and conversion are unknown. Formula owners should use official SARS material for each applicable assessment year and test edge cases, especially lifetime lump-sum history, eligibility-dependent deductions, ring-fenced losses, crypto revenue/capital classification, property fee estimates and changing 2026 versus 2027 thresholds. Public TaxTim copy itself is not the source of legal truth.
