CONFIRMED FINDINGS AND FIXES

F1 — P2, browser Back restored calculator amounts after leaving.
With Chromium's Playwright BFCache-disabling default removed, the reviewer entered fictional salary 51829.47 and a comparison, navigated to /sample as a full document, and pressed Back on hosted v4. pageshow.persisted was true; the original page marker, salary and Compare1 were restored. This directly contradicted the promise that leaving calculators clears their amounts. The provider now synchronously resets transient state on pagehide, resets again on a persisted pageshow, and changes a keyed child fragment so local result snapshots, names and messages also unmount. Favorites remain. The reviewer inspected the patch and independently verified all three local lifecycle paths, including non-persisted pageshow preserving an active session. Actual hosted retest is pending publication.

F2 — P2, deletion dismissal reused a prior confirmation and lost focus.
Escape/Cancel/X could leave DELETE MY PREPARATIONS populated on reopening, enabling deletion without fresh entry. Closing now clears the phrase, closes the native dialog and restores focus to its trigger after deletion/inert state finishes. Opening also starts blank. A failed deletion retains the phrase and dialog for retry. Sol's actual local success/retry probe passed; the reviewer independently verified Escape, Cancel, X, injected DELETE503, subsequent cancellation and 390px layout without mutating saved records.

F3 — P2, narrow result rows split a currency amount.
At 320px, the final cent digit of R360000,00 wrapped onto a separate line. Result rows now stack their labels and values at narrow widths. The coordinator checked actual text-line geometry and page overflow for normal and near-maximum salary values at 320, 390, 420, 768 and 1440px: all ten cases passed. The reviewer inspected the original failure and corrected narrow screenshots. Guide text remains allowed to wrap.
