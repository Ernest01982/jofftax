The [complete TaxTim calculator hub inventory](./calculator-market-research.md) has 33 calculator/diagnostic entries and three guidance tools. Its page says calculators are free without registration. Calculator breadth is therefore a **free product expectation**. Filing or practitioner service parity would require different operational and legal capabilities, not merely identical calculator categories.

## Commercial position to test

Make the free layer genuinely useful: all approved bounded calculations and guides, source-stamped explanations, assumptions and exclusions, editable scenarios, comparisons and export. Connect salary, bonus, retirement, investment, travel and evidence questions into a journey that prepares a person for an informed SARS decision. This is a proposed Joff experience, not evidence that competitors lack each feature. The official SARS channel remains the actual free filing option.

A proposed paid **reviewed filing-readiness service** could reconcile records and calculated scenarios into one assessment-year evidence packet, identify contradictions and missing proof, and provide a named accountable review with an action plan. It should state explicitly whether the customer self-files, a registered practitioner prepares/files, or Joff merely supplies a handoff packet. Those three outcomes have different staffing, authorisation, liability and pricing. A practitioner filing/audit response service cannot be represented as live until staffed, contracted and authorised. Generic document storage, reminder emails, AI answers or downloads alone are weak paywall candidates because TaxTim already advertises them free.

Test a clear fixed fee for a specific reviewed outcome rather than a percentage of a hoped-for refund. The earlier **R149 self-service pack / R249 annual preparation** ideas are superseded by [the current commercial blueprint](./commercial-delivery-blueprint.md): self-guided preparation remains free, while a **R499 reviewed readiness case** and **complex review from R999** are hypotheses to test only after real practitioner staffing, scope and operations exist. These are not payable tariffs or a current product promise. Actual filing would be a separately quoted, authorised service. Do not promise a refund, maximum deduction, favourable SARS decision or a completion time that depends on SARS.

Acquisition hypotheses: search-led entry through free calculator pages tied to concrete South African tax questions, useful deadline/checklist exports, and a return path from a result to saved preparation. Measure completed useful calculations, follow-on tool use, export, return visits, explicit paid-interest signals, actual conversion and human support minutes. No credible customer acquisition cost, conversion rate, market share, demand, retention or willingness-to-pay figure was established by this review. Avoid treating search snippets, testimonials or competitor claims as those measurements.
