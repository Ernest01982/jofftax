## Stage 3 personal evaluator, source read

`lib/calculator-personal.ts` observed after Stage 3 approval: the CGT signed current-year net, annual exclusion, prior capital-loss order and 40% inclusion match the [SARS ABC Guide](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Guides/Legal-Pub-Guide-CGT02-ABC-Guide-on-CGT-for-Individuals.pdf). TFSA shows headroom and annual-only 40% excess; a lifetime breach suppresses tax, as approved. Provisional first/second planning formulas use distinct first-period versus annual credit inputs. Standalone medical E>0 uses under65/65+ formulas and zero-floor tax without changing saved preparation. Full independent fixtures/integration remain verification's task.

Explicit supported boundaries: the CGT order applies to confirmed ordinary capital assets, not home/death/rollover cases; a 2027 current R30,000 gain with R100,000 prior assessed capital loss leaves that prior loss intact, whereas a current R60,000 capital loss carries R110,000. The TFSA tool must use all-provider *contributions*, including reinvested withdrawals, and withhold a combined tax figure for **any lifetime breach** without historic excess-penalty facts ([SARS TFSA](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-free-investments/)). Provisional amounts are planning components, not a penalty-safe IRP6. The basic amount needs the qualifying preceding assessment and statutory exclusions; uplift is linear 8% per applicable year. Medical E must be confirmed qualifying, paid and unreimbursed; section 11F uses reconciled pre-deduction income and contribution fringe benefits once ([SARS medical guide](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Guides/Legal-Pub-Guide-IT07-Guide-on-the-Determination-of-Medical-Tax-Credits.pdf); [SARS employer guide](https://www.sars.gov.za/guide-for-employers-in-respect-of-employees-tax-2027/)).

3. **Provisional-status threshold and source consistency resolved on source read:** no-business taxable income **does not exceed** age threshold; evaluator initially used `<` and now uses `<=`, as confirmed by [SARS provisional FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/faq/faq-who-is-exempt-from-provisional-tax/). This prevents a mixed registered/unregistered employer or taxable passive income from silently being called registered salary alone. Outcomes remain conditional.

## Period and authority status

The **2026 individual assessment** runs 1 March 2025–28 February 2026. Its tax table is enacted by [Act 3 of 2026](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Legal/AmendActs/Legal-LPrim-AA-2026-01-Rates-and-Monetary-Amounts-and-Amendment-of-Revenue-Laws-Act-3-of-2026-GG-54446-1-April-2026.pdf), whose Schedule I expressly applies to years beginning 1 March 2025. The **2027 assessment** runs 1 March 2026–28 February 2027; [SARS's current published table](https://www.sars.gov.za/tax-rates/income-tax/rates-of-tax-for-individuals/) and employer guidance inform its calculation, but the checked 2026 Rates Bill was [draft legislation](https://www.treasury.gov.za/legislation/draft_bills/2026%20Draft%20Rates%20Bill%20-%2025%20Feb%202026.pdf). Therefore 2027 individual income and uplifted limits remain labelled SARS-published forecast subject to enactment/final assessment in the product. In contrast, the [2027 vehicle cost scale](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/IncomeTaxNotices/Legal-LSec-IT-GN-2026-03-Budget-2026-Rate-per-kilometre-iro-motor-vehicles-27-February-2026.pdf) has a gazetted notice effective 1 March 2026, and transfer duty has a distinct acquisition-date table unchanged from 1 April 2026; do not mark either generically proposed. The [2026 ITR12 filing season](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/filing-season/) is separate from IRP6 periods. No 2027 ITR12 filing deadline was verified on the research date; the stale provisional guide's “29 February 2027” is impossible.
