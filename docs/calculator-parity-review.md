# Calculator coverage and mode review

Source inspection snapshot: 7 October 2026. This compares the current application registry and evaluator branches with the 36 live TaxTim hub destinations in [calculator-market-research.md](calculator-market-research.md). It is a source-level review, not browser acceptance, a tax opinion, or a claim that every TaxTim form field and filing function is reproduced. The verification agent owns execution and release evidence in [calculator-coverage-audit.md](calculator-coverage-audit.md).

## Entry coverage

The registry has 36 unique entries: eight initial definitions and 28 further entries. Each of the 28 has a matching live definition in `lib/calculator-families.ts`, `lib/calculator-personal.ts`, or `lib/calculator-specialists.ts`, so the fallback pending card is not selected in this source snapshot. The 29 general entries, four specialist entries and three guides are represented under the corresponding researched TaxTim slugs. Each definition has an evaluator branch. The three guides produce bounded calendar, document and self-reported refund guidance rather than a numeric tax result. An entry count cannot establish successful calculations, rendered navigation, visual quality or deployability; those are separate release gates.

## Distinct functions and current boundaries

| Area | Implemented source path | Material boundary compared with TaxTim's public tool description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salary and PAYE | Annual taxable-income tax, twelve equal salary months, bonus annual increment, bracket and refund comparison; hourly gross, actual annual net and full-pay-period withholding illustrations; target net and net-raise inversion | No general payslip engine for irregular remuneration, benefits, directives, retirement contributions or travel allowance. Bonus is an annual normal-tax increment, not actual employer PAYE. Refund is an indicative overpayment, not SARS account status. |
| UIF | Monthly employee/employer contributions; unemployment, certified illness and maternity benefit illustrations using stated eligibility, history and known days | No actual benefit ledger, claim adjudication or verified payout. Binding Act illness IRR/20% branch differs from maternity's 66% branch. |
| Retirement | Retirement/withdrawal lump-sum cumulative table, two-pot normal-tax increment, section 11F contribution tax saving and assumed balance growth, retrenchment severance and separate ordinary-income component | No SARS directive, debt offset or actual fund entitlement. Growth excludes tax, fees and future law. |
| Investments | Aggregate ordinary capital gains, crypto in explicitly established capital or revenue classification, local interest, restricted foreign-dividend component, donations tax and TFSA headroom/excess plus assumed growth | No property-specific primary-residence CGT calculation, estate/special relief, automatic crypto classification, disability case or comprehensive lifetime TFSA penalty. The TFSA growth mode does not compare a taxable investment account as TaxTim's public description does. |
| Work and property | Travel deemed and established actual-cost methods; company car benefit; rental known-profit component; transfer duty; VAT arithmetic; eligible SBC table; one-to-20-row payroll with confirmed SDL | Transfer-duty result excludes estimated conveyancing, bond and other transaction fees, so it is not total purchase cost. Rental loss is routed for ring-fencing review. Payroll is equal-month planning, not production PAYE. |
| Deductions | Medical scheme and known paid unreimbursed expenses; employee home-office premises allocation; selected new-asset section 11(e) lives; section 12C, 12E, 11(f) and 11(g) bounded schedules | Medical disability/impairment is excluded. Specialist asset/lease eligibility is asserted, with narrow classes and full-month periods; no claim certification. |
| Guidance | 2026 ITR12 event dates and separate 2027 IRP6 context, `.ics` voluntary calendar, situation-based records checklist and self-reported SARS refund next steps | No authenticated SARS status, automated reminders, document upload, confirmed future 2027 ITR12 calendar, or refund guarantee. |

The implemented mode breadth is materially greater than 36 placeholder cards: both retirement and TFSA growth, hourly net and period withholding, net raise, UIF benefits and the three branching guides have evaluator paths. Nevertheless, “all the same functionality as TaxTim” would overstate the evidence. TaxTim's paid return questionnaire, SARS data import, submission, practitioner help and filing checks are separate services from its free calculator hub and have not been implemented as Joff Tax services. Public pricing and feature facts are in the market inventory; the current proposed Joff commercial model is controlled by [commercial-delivery-blueprint.md](commercial-delivery-blueprint.md).
