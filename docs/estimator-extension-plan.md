# Bounded salary estimator extension

Prepared 7 October 2026 SAST. Status: **conditional implementation approval in `astra-calculation-amendment.md`**; Astra also approved this companion's input semantics subject to that amendment and code/tests. This is a UI/input-semantics companion to that controlling approval. It permits only reconciled current-year section 11F contributions and a registered South African medical scheme with no qualifying out-of-pocket/impairment expenses, disability or shared payer. Exact formulas, fixtures and enablement gates in the amendment control. No other scope expands. Base coding continues; extension results remain disabled/preparation-only until implemented gates and tests pass code review. Source qualifications remain in `sars-research.md`; this is a private estimate, not a practitioner opinion or SARS assessment.

## Eligibility and input meanings

Retain every base eligibility screen and explicit unanswered/yes/no/unsure semantics. Permit the previously blocked retirement/medical answers only when **all applicable extension confirmations are affirmative and reconciled**. Server, UI, saved review and export use the same eligibility function. Any unknown, missing confirmation or excluded circumstance produces preparation-only with a named blocker and evidence tasks. No default assumptions, auto-reconciliation or certificate-code-to-income mapping.

Common exclusions remain: non-residency or changed/partial-year residency; multiple employers; foreign or other income (including interest); capital gains; rental/trade; other deductions or losses; donations; travel/car/home-office/commission benefits or deductions; directives; lump sums, severance and two-pot/other withdrawals; prior assessment adjustments; any other taxable fringe benefit besides the explicitly reconciled employer medical/retirement contributions. User confirms a full ordinary assessment year. 2027 uses entered **full-year projected** totals and the existing forecast/legal-status notice, never year-to-date values treated as final.

| UI field or confirmation | Exact meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Assessment year and age band | Existing 2026/2027 period; age at 28 February of that assessment year, not today's age |
| `X`: annual taxable employment before retirement deduction | Ordinary cash employment and taxable bonus plus taxable employer pension/provident/RA and medical contribution benefits **already included once**. No retirement deduction has yet been subtracted. In this bounded case, this is both eligible remuneration and taxable income before section 11F, excluding CGT. It is neither take-home pay nor already-adjusted taxable income. |
| Income reconciliation confirmation | "I have checked my employment records. This amount includes taxable employer retirement and medical contributions once, has no retirement deduction removed, and is the same before-retirement figure for my remuneration and taxable income." If unsure, block. Do not claim an IRP5 total alone establishes this. |
| `C`: current-year eligible retirement contributions | Combined eligible South African pension, provident and RA contributions personally paid plus employer contributions deemed paid by the employee, **each counted once**. Input may be zero. No transferred amounts, prior-year excess/carryovers, withdrawals, arrears requiring reconciliation, uncertain/non-qualifying funds or amounts already deducted from `X`. |
| Retirement reconciliation confirmation | "These are only this year's eligible contributions, including employer contributions once. I have no prior-year excess, transfers or withdrawals, and none has already been deducted from the employment amount above." Unknown totals or a mismatch block. |
| Registered scheme confirmation | Contributions are to a registered South African medical scheme. No foreign fund, unverified statutory scheme or health-insurance product. No member names or diagnosis is collected. |
| `F`: annual eligible medical scheme fees | Personally paid eligible fees plus employer-paid fees treated as the employee's taxable contribution benefit, **counted once**. Do not add an employer amount again if already in the annual contribution total. Annual totals refer to the selected year. |
| Medical payer confirmation | "For these covered people, only I and my employer paid the fees; there is no spouse, family member or other payer whose payments require the tax credit to be shared." Employer deemed contributions are within this taxpayer's total; any true shared payer/apportionment blocks. |
| 12 monthly covered-person counts | Explicit March-to-February counts of eligible people covered in months with eligible contributions actually paid (2026) or paid/projected for the full year (2027). First and second covered people use the year-specific first-person credit, additional eligible dependants use the additional-person credit. Zero for a month without eligible actual/projected contributions. Allow variable membership; never copy year-end family size to all months automatically. No names/ages/medical details of dependants. |
| `E=0` confirmation | "I had no qualifying out-of-pocket medical or impairment expenses paid and borne by me that were not recoverable from a scheme or insurer." This must be affirmative; any expenses or uncertainty blocks the extension. Refunds or uncertainty about fee/credit entitlement also block. Do not collect an amount and silently ignore it. |
| No disability confirmation | Ask "Could a qualifying disability or impairment apply to you, your spouse, child or another dependant?" Any yes/unsure blocks; do not ask merely whether the user intends to make a claim. No diagnostic details are collected. |
| Annual PAYE | Tax already withheld by the single employer for the selected full year, or explicitly projected full-year withholding for 2027. Empty differs from zero. |

Question copy must explain the pre-deduction and "counted once" requirements next to inputs. For 2027, **every amount/month and confirmation uses full-year projection wording**, including retirement totals and projected benefit inclusion, expected eligible paid medical months/fees and expected `E=0`; it must not claim a completed certificate or a fact for an unelapsed month. A confirmation is a user assertion, not validation of a certificate or tax eligibility. Review/export repeat `X`, `C`, allowed deduction, `F`, monthly counts, all assumptions and their user-reported status. If no retirement or medical contribution exists, explicit zero/no confirmations preserve the base result.

## Calculation and validation

Implement exactly the section 11F, MTC and both AMTC regimes in `astra-calculation-amendment.md`. Show actual contribution `C`, allowable deduction `D`, taxable income `T`, MTC `M` and AMTC `A` separately. Excess retirement contributions are "not deducted in this estimate", without promising future carryover. Use **33.3%**, not an exact one-third, for the 65+ AMTC category. Both credits are non-refundable. Retain the amendment's 2027 cap/rates legislative caveat and illustrative PAYE language.

Validate strict bounded finite nonnegative monetary inputs and explicit integer monthly counts within documented reasonable limits. Reject incomplete 12-month arrays, unexpected enums/fields and inconsistent zero/positive fee/count assertions rather than inventing a correction. Preserve calculation precision; round presentation to cents and disclose that SARS rounding/other balances may differ. Persist new schema/rules provenance; existing cases require completing the new questions, with no newly assumed confirmations.

## Approval and verification gates

The recorded amendment is the explicit scope approval. Any material broadening returns to Astra. Approval of scope still requires code review and passing independent fixtures before enabling totals. Use all seven fixtures and the boundary matrix in `astra-calculation-amendment.md`, with supporting examples in `sars-research.md`; do not derive expected answers from the implementation's constants. The research example with `E=20000` is outside this extension and must block. Tests include the retirement deduction changing the under-65 AMTC income hurdle.

Eligibility tests exercise employee/employer amounts counted once, pre-deducted income/unknown reconciliation, shared medical payer, foreign/non-scheme health product, unknown paid months, missing confirmations, disability, nonzero/unknown E, carryovers/withdrawals and all unchanged base exclusions. Crafted server requests cannot bypass those screens. Dashboard/export must suppress overall numbers on any failed condition. Use fictional data only.

Build/type/fixture/repository tests provide implementation evidence. Browser usability, live ChatGPT sessions and deployed D1 checks remain separately recorded as passed/failed/pending under `astra-plan-review.md`. Enabling this extension does not change the private-preview-for-validation release wording or any paid-launch, practitioner, privacy or SARS-integration gate.

Official source links, exact formulas, rates, legal status and independent arithmetic are recorded in `astra-calculation-amendment.md` and `sars-research.md`. These documents are the shared reviewer/coder references.
