# Joff Tax commercial delivery blueprint

7 October 2026 SAST. **Astra Stage 3 approval** in `astra-calculator-plan-review.md` permits this proposed model and service-page explanation. It does not authorise checkout, booking, actual service delivery, filing or paid launch. Demand, staffing, practitioner registration and launch readiness are not claimed. The calculator expansion and all live preparation features remain free. Paid capabilities below are proposed until contracted people, workflows and launch gates exist. This supplements `calculator-expansion-plan.md`; Astra review does not substitute for South African tax/legal review.

## Customer and offer

Start with employed adults who have one IRP5 plus common retirement/medical certificates and want an independent check before accepting an auto-assessment or filing themselves. They are a better initial operational fit than broad crypto/foreign/rental disputes. Free calculators attract useful intent; the paid outcome is a **named practitioner's checked, reconciled tax-year pack and specific next actions**, rather than access to numbers already available free elsewhere.

Second segment, after the process is proven: an employed person who has added rental/freelance/investment income and needs scoped help resolving complexity. Existing calculators and screening must identify unsupported overall cases; they do not automatically prepare a complete return. Those cases require a quote and suitably qualified human review before a service commitment. Employer financial-wellness sponsorship is a later distribution test, not an existing partner.

| Offer | Concrete deliverable | Price hypothesis and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Joff Tools | All approved calculators/guides, comparisons, explanations, exports and existing preparation workspace | R0; no export or calculator paywall |
| Reviewed Readiness | One assessment year, one employment certificate, medical/retirement reconciliation within agreed scope; checked summary, discrepancies/evidence gaps, recommended next actions and one defined clarification round | Test **R499 per year/case**. Customer submits in SARS themselves. No refund promise or submission included. |
| Complex Review | Written scope/quote for added income/assets, a qualified review and clear handover; no automatic acceptance of every case | Test **from R999**, quoted before work; price/scope validated with practitioner capacity |
| Assisted Filing | Actual contracted registered practitioner or authorised integration prepares, obtains customer approval, submits and returns genuine receipt | Separate future service and quote; no current checkout or submission claim |

These are price experiments, not published payable tariffs. Earlier self-guided Prep Pack/annual-readiness prices in `product-plan.md` are hypotheses superseded for this expansion by **free self-guided functionality plus reviewed paid outcomes**. Do not charge for the current calculator/export/preparation experience. Do not present a recurring subscription until repeated benefit and retention are demonstrated. Current primary-source competitor observations in `market-research.md` include basic tax services below R499 as well as TaxTim's higher expert-filing offer. That makes a R499 readiness-only offer a demanding hypothesis: validate a clearly differentiated checked outcome or lower/restructure the offer rather than implying the customer should pay more for less service. Recheck live scope and tariffs before publishing comparisons; filing/practitioner services differ from readiness-only review.

## Service process to build before charging

1. Free diagnosis identifies the year, case type, uncertainty and evidence needs. The customer sees exactly what self-guided work is complete, which items remain, and a sample structure of the future review deliverable. A current UI button can genuinely create the free preparation pack; it must not claim to book a reviewer.
2. Before purchase, a real reviewer confirms supported scope, document list, quoted price, responsibility for submission, clarification allowance, expected turnaround and cancellation/refund terms. No unsupported case is sold through a generic automatic checkout.
3. With lawful notice/consent and secure document controls established, the customer supplies the agreed records. The service reconciles employment/payments/contributions and checks calculation assumptions; no SARS password/OTP is collected. A named registered professional owns the review and records decisions.
4. The customer receives a reviewed pack with a checked-input reconciliation, discrepancies, evidence gaps, relevant calculation/source provenance and an actionable self-filing or practitioner handover. It distinguishes completed checks from matters outside scope.
5. The customer approves corrections and uses the defined clarification round. Any filing, correspondence response or extra-year service needs explicit separately quoted scope. Actual filing is never inferred from completion of a pack. Record service completion, complaints/refunds and permitted retention/deletion.

Before launch, make a fictional completed deliverable from this structure and have prospective customers assess it. The paid preview should show the concrete outcome and process, alongside a working **"Build my free readiness pack"** action. Use "Proposed review service" and truthful staffing status. No invented practitioner portraits, customer quotes, trust badges, successful booking, waitlist submission or service SLA. A feedback form may be offered only if its storage/action is really implemented and its purpose is disclosed.

## Economics and validation decisions

Illustrative unit model, entirely assumed: R499 revenue; 30 minutes reviewer time at R300/hour = R150; 15 minutes support/admin at R200/hour = R50; assumed payment cost 3% plus R3 = R17.97; R25 variable hosting/document/operations allocation. Contribution before acquisition, fixed overhead and taxes is **R256.03**, about 51%. These are budgeting inputs, not observed costs or available supplier prices. They exclude VAT treatment; confirm legal status and tax-inclusive pricing before taking payment. If reviewer time doubles, margin falls materially. Actual practitioner quotes, processing fees, support time and refunds decide viability.

Validation sequence and stop/go criteria:

- Interview 10 to 15 target salary users about their latest assessment/preparation, then test the free flow on a realistic fictional case. At least 8 should independently find the relevant tool and understand the result's limits without researcher intervention.
- Show the concrete reviewed pack and scope, then test R399/R499/R699 offers without charging or pretending fulfillment. Record purchase intent, reason, urgency and whether readiness-only is understood. Seek at least 5 credible commitments to a limited pilot; this is not conversion proof.
- Once launch gates pass, run a small paid pilot with a contracted reviewer and explicit service terms. Measure completed reviews, actual minutes/case, corrections, clarification rounds, support load, refunds and customer-reported usefulness. Collect testimonials only with permission; do not invent success rates.
- Continue only if real contribution margin after support/refunds is positive, the reviewed outcome is valued separately from free tools, and cases can be delivered safely within tested capacity. If customers primarily want submission, validate an actual authorised filing service rather than obscuring the readiness scope.

Initial acquisition experiments: useful search landing pages connecting specific questions to tools; practitioner referrals for better client preparation; small employer financial-wellness pilots when agreed. Measure cost per qualified supported case and completed paid review, not raw traffic. No partnership, search ranking, acquisition cost or recurring revenue is currently established.

## Operational acceptance before paid/public launch

The responsible legal seller, named registered practitioners/contracts, competent supported-case matrix, document intake/access/retention controls, privacy notices/lawful processing/operator arrangements, independent security review and incident handling must exist and be reviewed. Define capacity, escalation, customer communication, complaints/refund policy and cancellation terms; test the service using fictional cases first. No compliance claim follows from a generated policy.

Only then implement an appropriate payment provider with server-created orders, validated/idempotent webhooks, a scope-specific entitlement, genuine invoices, reconciliation and tested refunds. Obtain tax/legal review of pricing and consumer terms. Filing requires a separate practitioner/authorised integration gate, consent, audit trail, customer approval and genuine SARS receipts. The present private preview has no payment or operational-service readiness conferred by this blueprint.
