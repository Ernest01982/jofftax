# Joff Tax calculator rule contracts

Official primary-source research checked **7 October 2026 (SAST)**. These contracts cover the public TaxTim calculator inventory recorded in [calculator-market-research.md](./calculator-market-research.md). They define bounded estimates, not a return, assessment, tax directive, payroll engine, entitlement decision or SARS submission. Every result must show the selected assessment or transaction period, assumptions, source/review date and a refusal or preparation-only path when a required fact is unknown. Never infer eligibility from a numerical amount alone.

## Version and shared calculation basis

* **2026 individual assessment:** 1 March 2025–28 February 2026; **2027:** 1 March 2026–28 February 2027. The 2026 rates are in [Act 3 of 2026, Schedule I](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Legal/AmendActs/Legal-LPrim-AA-2026-01-Rates-and-Monetary-Amounts-and-Amendment-of-Revenue-Laws-Act-3-of-2026-GG-54446-1-April-2026.pdf), expressly for years beginning 1 March 2025. SARS's [2027 individual rates](https://www.sars.gov.za/tax-rates/income-tax/rates-of-tax-for-individuals/) and [2027 employer guide](https://www.sars.gov.za/guide-for-employers-in-respect-of-employees-tax-2027/) are published and used for payroll from 1 March 2026; a [2026 Draft Rates Bill](https://www.treasury.gov.za/legislation/draft_bills/2026%20Draft%20Rates%20Bill%20-%2025%20Feb%202026.pdf) was the legislative material checked. Label 2027 rates and uplifted monetary limits **SARS-published Budget estimate, subject to enactment and final assessment** until a later Act is verified. Do not cite the 2026 Act as enactment of 2027 limits.
* Full individual brackets, rebates, medical and retirement rule notes and four detailed medical fixtures are in [sars-research.md](./sars-research.md). To avoid a second divergent configuration, consume one shared immutable year table. Ordinary tax is `max(0, bracketTax(taxableIncome) − ageRebates − nonrefundableMedicalCredits)`. Credits/rebates cannot generate cash. Round monetary display only after calculating, subject to SARS assessment rounding. The 2026 under-65 primary rebate is R17,235 and first bracket upper bound R237,100; 2027 R17,820 and R245,100. Age is age at assessment end, not today's age.
* `incrementalNormalTax(additionalTaxableIncome)` means `ordinaryTax(baseTaxableIncome + additionalTaxableIncome) − ordinaryTax(baseTaxableIncome)` under the **same** year, age, deductions and medical assumptions. This is an estimate of assessment impact, not automatically PAYE withheld. Input base taxable income must exclude the increment, be before/after section 11F consistently, and include every other source; otherwise decline the incremental result.
* No calculator may silently combine ordinary income with separately taxed retirement/withdrawal/severance tables, treat PAYE as final assessment tax, or report a guaranteed refund. Never project an unpublished 2027 ITR12 filing deadline.

For a 12-month, constant, no-other-income illustration at R30,000 per month, taxable annual salary R360,000, no medical/RA: 2026 bracket tax `42,678 + .26×(360,000−237,100)=74,632`, less rebate R17,235 = **R57,397**, annualized monthly income tax R4,783.0833; 2027 `44,118 + .26×(360,000−245,100)=73,992`, less R17,820 = **R56,172**, monthly R4,681. A separately paid R30,000 annual bonus raises the estimate to R66,172 (2026: increment **R8,775**) or R64,317 (2027: increment **R8,145**), because it crosses into the 31% band in each year. These are independently derived full-year liability illustrations. By contrast, if employed for **only three full months** at R30,000 with no other income, actual assessment ordinary salary R90,000 has zero tax after primary rebate, but the employer's year-equivalent R360,000 withholding illustration is `57,397×3/12=R14,349.25` for 2026 or `56,172×3/12=R14,043` for 2027. The difference is precisely why a PAYE figure is not a final assessment or promised refund. Employer actual table rounding can differ.

Fixtures: a 2026 individual sells one ordinary non-home capital asset with a verified R200,000 capital gain, no other gains/losses, and no carried loss: capital inclusion `(200,000−40,000)×.4 = R64,000`; the **tax due is still dependent on the other taxable income**. For 2027 the analogous inclusion is R60,000. Counterexample: 2027 current capital gain R30,000 and prior assessed capital loss R100,000: the R50,000 annual exclusion absorbs all R30,000, so included gain **R0** and the prior assessed loss remains **R100,000**. With a current capital **loss** R60,000 instead, the R50,000 annual exclusion reduces current loss to R10,000 and the assessed capital loss carried forward becomes R110,000. A non-VAT property taxable value R2,000,000 under the current transfer table gives `13,614 + .06×(2,000,000−1,663,800) = R33,786` duty. VAT on R1,150 inclusive is R150. A 2027 TFSA contribution of R50,000, with prior lifetime contributions at most R450,000, gives annual excess R4,000, no lifetime breach and illustrative excess tax R1,600. A simultaneous-cap diagnostic example `L=R490,000`, `A=R50,000` shows annual excess R4,000 and lifetime excess R40,000 but **blocks the combined tax amount** because reaching R490,000 before 2027 required historic annual excess and prior penalty history. A 2027 natural person's R200,000 gift, no earlier gifts this year or since 2018, no special exemption: R150,000 annual exemption, R50,000 taxed at 20% = R10,000.

## Travel, vehicle, UIF and ancillary tools

Travel allowance, per-kilometre reimbursement, company-car fringe benefits and UIF contribution/benefit calculations have separate detailed source contracts in [travel-payroll-contracts.md](./travel-payroll-contracts.md) and [uif-benefit-contract.md](./uif-benefit-contract.md), subject to Astra's later controlling amendment. The travel contract includes official 2026/2027 cost bands, logbook/days/fuel/maintenance gates and a documented 2027 discrepancy (gazetted Notice and eLogbook **126.9** cents/km versus employer annex **126.1** for the upper maintenance band). The vehicle contract asks for already determined vehicle value and maintenance-plan status. A narrow UIF contribution illustration is 1% of eligible remuneration for employee and 1% employer, each capped at R177.12 per month on R17,712 ([SARS UIF](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/unemployment-insurance-fund/)). For benefit modes, [binding UIF Act](https://www.gov.za/documents/unemployment-insurance-act) and Astra's final gate control: unemployment and **illness** use income replacement rate 38–60% for first 238 credited days, then 20% for days 239–365; illness is additionally capped by actual unpaid wage shortfall. **Maternity** has a distinct 66% capped rate and wage-shortfall limit. Some Department explanatory materials group illness with 66%, contradicting the statutory calculation; the earlier grouped 66% illness statement is withdrawn pending any legally effective contrary notice. The Department's **one credit day per four completed contribution days** also conflicts with TaxTim's five-day statement; use official guidance. Do not invent a flat travel-kilometre cost or apply a company-car percentage without determined value and vehicle eligibility.

The three noncalculator public entries are actionable tools with boundaries: **tax deadlines** shows the [official 2026 ITR12 dates](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/filing-season/) (nonprovisional 23 October 2026, provisional 22 January 2027; auto-assessment 1–12 July 2026) and verified provisional calendar only; 2027 ITR12 season `unknown`. **Tax-return documents** is a conditional checklist based on income/medical/retirement/rental/CGT flags, with [SARS ITR12 source guide](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Guides/IT-AE-36-G05-Comprehensive-Guide-to-the-ITR12-Income-Tax-Return-for-Individuals-External-Guide.pdf); no claim that possession requires filing. **Where is my SARS refund** links to [SARS's own refund-status channels](https://www.sars.gov.za/latest-news/how-to-check-your-tax-refund-status/) and directs a person to the [Income Tax Statement of Account](https://www.sars.gov.za/faq/faq-where-do-i-find-my-statement-of-account-itsa-to-see-my-outstanding-balance/) for actual amount/payment date; do not infer approval or payment timing from an estimated balance and do not collect eFiling passwords/OTPs.
